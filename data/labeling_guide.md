# Headline topic classification — labelling guide

Assign one primary editorial category per headline, based on its central subject.

- **World:** government, international relations, diplomacy, conflicts, elections and public affairs.
- **Sports:** sports teams, athletes, competition, leagues and tournaments.
- **Business:** companies, earnings, finance, markets, consumers and the economy.
- **Sci/Tech:** scientific research, engineering, digital products, computing and discoveries.

## Ambiguity rules
- A technology company's **earnings** announcement is **Business**, not Sci/Tech.
- Government discussion of **technology regulation** is **World** if regulation is the primary subject.
- A sports club's **takeover or financing** is **Business** if the transaction is the main event.
- If multiple categories are plausible, choose the dominant topic and note uncertainty.

For independent human validation, do not reveal the reference category before annotation.
